Theorem 1: Seismic wave velocities: P-waves faster than S-waves

Statement: Theorem 1: Seismic wave velocities: P-waves faster than S-waves

Hypotheses: Given assumptions in seismology

Conclusion: The stated property holds

Definitions: fundamental definitions in seismology

Proof sketch:

Step 1: P-wave velocity: V_p = sqrt((K + 4mu/3) / rho).

Step 2: S-wave velocity: V_s = sqrt(mu / rho).

Step 3: Since K > 0 and mu > 0, we have K + 4mu/3 > mu, so V_p > V_s.

Step 4: In fluids: mu = 0 (no shear resistance), so V_s = 0.

Step 5: This is why S-wave shadow zone exists in Earth's liquid outer core.
