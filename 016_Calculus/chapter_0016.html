Theorem 1: MVT: f continuous on [a,b], diff on (a,b) => f'(c) = (f(b)-f(a))/(b-a)

Statement: Theorem 1: MVT: f continuous on [a,b], diff on (a,b) => f'(c) = (f(b)-f(a))/(b-a)

Hypotheses: Given assumptions in calculus

Conclusion: The stated property holds

Definitions: fundamental definitions in calculus

Proof sketch:

Step 1: Define g(x) = f(x) - f(a) - [(f(b)-f(a))/(b-a)]*(x-a).

Step 2: Then g(a) = g(b) = 0, g continuous on [a,b], differentiable on (a,b).

Step 3: By Rolle's theorem, there exists c in (a,b) with g'(c) = 0.

Step 4: Compute g'(c) = f'(c) - (f(b)-f(a))/(b-a) = 0.

Step 5: Therefore f'(c) = (f(b)-f(a))/(b-a).
