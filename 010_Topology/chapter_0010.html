Theorem 1: Brouwer fixed point: continuous f: D^n -> D^n has fixed point

Statement: Brouwer fixed point: continuous f: D^n -> D^n has fixed point

Hypotheses: D^n closed unit disk, f: D^n -> D^n continuous

Conclusion: f has fixed point

Definitions: Brouwer, fixed point, disk

Proof sketch:

Step 1: Assume f has no fixed point

Step 2: Define r(x) = point on S^{n-1} on ray from f(x) through x

Step 3: r is a continuous retraction: D^n -> S^{n-1}

Step 4: But no such retraction exists (Brouwer non-retraction theorem)

Step 5: Contradiction

Hypotheses: X compact Hausdorff, A,B disjoint closed subsets

Conclusion: disjoint open U,V with A subset U, B subset V

Definitions: compact, Hausdorff, normal, separation

Proof sketch:

Step 1: For each a in A, by Hausdorff separate from each b in B

Step 6: Let U = union, V = intersection of complements

Definitions: invariance of dimension, topological invariant

Proof sketch:
