Einstein field equations: spacetime geometry determined by matter-energy

Statement: G_{mu nu} + Lambda g_{mu nu} = 8*pi*G/c^4 * T_{mu nu}

Hypotheses: spacetime manifold, energy-momentum tensor

Conclusion: geometry determined by matter distribution

Definitions: Einstein tensor, cosmological constant, stress-energy tensor

Physical law (described, not derived):

Step 1: Einstein tensor G = Ric - (1/2)Rg encodes spacetime curvature

Step 2: Right side encodes matter-energy content

Step 3: Lambda = cosmological constant (dark energy)

Step 4: Ten coupled nonlinear PDEs govern spacetime geometry

Schwarzschild metric: exact solution for spherically symmetric vacuum

Statement: ds^2 = -(1-r_s/r)c^2 dt^2 + dr^2/(1-r_s/r) + r^2 dOmega^2, r_s = 2GM/c^2

Hypotheses: spherically symmetric vacuum solution

Conclusion: spacetime around non-rotating black hole

Definitions: Schwarzschild, event horizon, black hole

Proof sketch:

Step 1: Solve R_{mu nu} = 0 with spherical symmetry

Step 2: Unique solution parameterized by mass M

Step 3: Event horizon at r = 2GM/c^2

Step 4: Birkhoff theorem guarantees uniqueness
