Proof sketch:

Step 1: Ohm's law: V = IR relates voltage (V), current (I), and resistance (R).

Step 2: Empirically determined by Georg Ohm (1827) through systematic measurements on wires.

Step 3: Microscopic: J = sigma * E where J is current density, sigma is conductivity, E is electric field.

Step 4: In circuit form: V = IR for a resistor with resistance R at constant temperature.

Step 5: Limitations: breaks down for semiconductors (non-linear I-V), superconductors (R=0), and high frequencies.
