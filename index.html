12-Step Contract (for every proof)

  1. Stable ID - Unique hash identifier
  2. Subject area - Mathematical/scientific domain
  3. Definitions - All concepts used in the theorem
  4. Exact statement - Precise mathematical formulation
  5. Hypotheses - All required conditions
  6. Conclusion - What is proved
  7. Dependencies - Other theorems used
  8. Step-by-step proof - Complete logical argument
  9. Lemma verification - Checking intermediate results
  10. Formal status - UNFORMALIZED / LEAN_DRAFT / LEAN_BUILDS
  11. Mutation test - What happens when hypotheses removed
  12. Provenance - Source and history of the theorem
