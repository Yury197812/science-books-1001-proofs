EMPIRICAL_LAW: Seismic wave velocities: P-waves faster than S-waves

Statement: Seismic wave velocities: P-waves faster than S-waves

Hypotheses: elastic medium, earthquake source

Conclusion: Vp > Vs always

Definitions: P-wave, S-wave, seismic

Proof sketch:

Step 1: P-wave speed: Vp = sqrt((K + 4/3 mu)/rho)

Step 2: S-wave speed: Vs = sqrt(mu/rho)

Step 3: K > 0 (bulk modulus), so Vp > Vs

Step 4: S-waves cannot travel through liquids (mu=0)
